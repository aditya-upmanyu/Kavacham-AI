# Category 4 — Looks Fake But Is Actually Genuine

🔥🔥 This is the reverse adversarial test.

These mails deliberately use words that normally trigger spam detection but are completely legitimate.

### Mail 1 — Real Security Alert

Subject: URGENT SECURITY ALERT – New Login Detected

Hi Aditya,

This is a legitimate security notification from the university IT department.

A new login to your institutional account was detected from a device that has not previously been registered.

If you recognize this activity, no action is required.

If you do not recognize it, contact the IT help desk through the official university contact information.

We will never ask you to provide your password, OTP or recovery code by email.

Regards,
University IT Security Team

### Mail 2 — Real Payment Confirmation

Subject: PAYMENT CONFIRMATION – Registration Successfully Completed

Dear Aditya,

Your registration payment of ₹500 has been successfully received and recorded in our system.

This email is only a payment confirmation for your records.

No further payment is required.

Please do not share your card number, password or OTP with anyone claiming to represent the organization.

Regards,
Accounts Department

### Mail 3 — Real Prize/Event Announcement

Subject: Congratulations – Your Project Has Been Selected

Dear Aditya,

Congratulations! Your project has been selected for the final presentation round of the university technology competition.

There is no registration fee, payment requirement or purchase associated with this selection.

The final presentation schedule and judging criteria are available through the official university event portal.

Please bring your project demonstration on the scheduled date.

Regards,
Competition Organizing Committee

### Mail 4 — Real Workshop

Subject: LIMITED SEATS – Cybersecurity Workshop Registration

Dear Students,

Registration is now open for the upcoming cybersecurity awareness workshop.

The workshop will cover phishing detection, password security, malware awareness and incident reporting.

Seats are limited to 50 participants. Registration is completely free and will close once all seats are filled.

This is an official academic workshop organized by the department.

Regards,
Training & Development Cell

## 📊 How to evaluate your AI

Use a matrix like this:

| Category                | Actual | Expected AI |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ----------- |
| 1 — Genuine             | Ham    | 🟢 Ham      |
| 2 — Obvious Spam        | Spam   | 🔴 Spam     |
| 3 — Looks Genuine, Spam | Spam   | 🔴 Spam     |
| 4 — Looks Fake, Genuine | Ham    | 🟢 Ham      |

The most valuable metrics for your project are:

**Category 1:** False Positive check
**Category 2:** Basic Spam detection
**Category 3:** False Negative / sophisticated spam check
**Category 4:** Adversarial False Positive check

For a Naive Bayes project, **Category 3 + Category 4 are the real stress tests** because they test whether your model has learned patterns/context rather than simply memorizing words like `urgent`, `security`, `payment`, `verify`, `winner`, and `limited`.
