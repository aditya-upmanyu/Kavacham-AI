NEW INVESTIGATION

Open a case record with evidence, indicators and assignment.

1CASE INFORMATION
2INITIAL EVIDENCE
3INDICATORS
4ASSIGNMENT
5CONFIRMATION
STEP 1 — CASE INFORMATION
Required. Maximum 200 characters.
Optional. Stored in the case record and audit trail.
STEP 2 — INITIAL EVIDENCE

Record the subject line or a short description of the first evidence item. Full evidence acquisition happens in the Evidence Vault after the case is opened.

Optional. Left blank means no evidence recorded at creation.
STEP 3 — INDICATORS (IOC)

One indicator per line. Types (IP, URL, DOMAIN, EMAIL, MD5, SHA1, SHA256) are derived from the literal value — severity is not inferred.

Optional. Blank lines are ignored.
STEP 4 — ASSIGNMENT
Optional. Recorded on the case and its timeline.
STEP 5 — CONFIRMATION

Review before opening the record. On creation the system generates the case ID, records the timestamp, writes an audit event and initializes the timeline.
